# Tonkotsu ramen, lifted high

A photorealistic close-up of a bowl of tonkotsu ramen at the moment
the noodles are lifted: glossy strands, a swirl of chili oil, jammy
eggs, and charred pork, with steam rising against a dark, warm
background.

## Approach

The picture was made as a photographic food portrait, then cropped
square and resized to 1024x1024 so the pull, the eggs, and the chili
swirl all read clearly even at thumbnail size. The entry ships the
finished pixels as-is; no text is drawn on the image.

## Files

- `entry.png` — the 1024x1024 food photograph (JPEG bytes)
- `main.py` — copies `entry.png` to the output directory, deterministically
